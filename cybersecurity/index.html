Cybersecurity

Report a vulnerability

If you find a weakness in any Pauhu site or service, tell us. We follow coordinated disclosure: you report to us first, we fix it, and only then is it made public.

Send your report to this address. Good-faith reports under this policy are not pursued.

security@pauhu.eu

Please include:

  • what you found and where,
  • the steps to reproduce it,
  • what you think the impact is.

Please do not:

  • read, change or keep other people's data,
  • disrupt the service or run denial-of-service tests,
  • share the details publicly before we have had a chance to fix it.

What happens after a report

  • We confirm that we received it.
  • We assess it and tell you what we found.
  • We fix it, and we tell you when it is fixed.
  • If you wish, we credit you by the name you choose.

Our targets: we acknowledge a report within 72 hours and assess its severity within 7 days. Public disclosure is agreed with you, by default after 90 days.
