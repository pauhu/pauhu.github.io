Accessibility

We want this site to work for everyone. That means people and machines. This statement covers pauhu.dev.

Our standard

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 at level AA.

How far we are

This site partially conforms to WCAG 2.2 AA. On 31 October 2026 an automated scan of its page at phone and desktop width found no failures. Automated scans cover only part of the standard.

What we are still improving

  • Colour contrast over images, keyboard use and focus order have not yet been fully checked by hand.

What works for you now

  • Pages have a skip link and a page language.
  • Pages fit a phone screen without sideways scrolling.

Tell us

If something is hard to use, or you need content in another form, tell us the page and what went wrong. We will reply and fix it.
